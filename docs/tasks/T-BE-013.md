# T-BE-013 — Student story reading APIs

**Status:** done
**Module:** `src/modules/content`
**Depends on:** T-BE-012, T-BE-006
**Priority:** P0

## Goal

An active student can browse published stories and open a chapter’s pages and audio.

## Scope

Student routes use `JwtAuthGuard`, `RolesGuard` (`STUDENT`), and `ActiveStudentGuard`. Lists only `PUBLISHED` stories in the student’s school, not soft-deleted. Query: `category`, `ageGroup`, `search` (title contains), `page`, `perPage`.

Detail includes chapters and, when a `ReadingProgress` row exists, its percent. Progress **writes** are T-BE-016. This task only reads progress if the row is there.

Chapter payload: ordered pages (`body_text`, `image_url`) and `audio_url` when `ChapterAudio` exists. Draft stories → 404.

## Out of scope

- `PUT` progress and chapter complete (T-BE-016).
- Quizzes.
- Audio-books or Kindness Corner student routes (no P0 screen).

## Notes

Student routes use `JwtAuthGuard`, `RolesGuard` (`STUDENT`), and `ActiveStudentGuard`. A student who is not `ACTIVE` gets 422 with message `PENDING`.

The library returns `PUBLISHED` stories in the student’s school and passes `deleted_at: null`. Drafts and other schools are excluded. `search` is a case-insensitive title contains. `category` and `ageGroup` filter when sent. A draft, a missing story, or another school’s story is 404 on detail and on the chapter route. A chapter id that is not on that story is 404.

Story detail lists chapters as id, number, title, and page count. Page text is only on the chapter route, as `number`, `bodyText`, and `imageUrl`, plus `audioUrl` when audio exists. If a `ReadingProgress` row exists it is returned as percent, chapter id, and page number. This task does not write progress.
